Why “Alfa Omega”?

Alfa is the beginning and Omega is the end. We take that seriously: we stay responsible for your site, your people and your event from the first minute until the last.

Alfa Omega Bohir Bisho Security Group is built in Dubai, a city of towers, events and international business. Our shield-and-skyline emblem reflects that. We combine disciplined guards, clear reporting and quick communication so you always know your property is covered.

What you can expect

Security works when the basics are done every day. These are the standards we hold our teams to.

  • Trained and screened guards

    Every guard is background-checked and briefed on your site before the first shift.

  • Clear daily reporting

    Incident and shift reports are shared with your management in plain language.

  • Fast response

    A supervisor is always reachable, and replacements are arranged without gaps in coverage.

  • Respect for local rules

    We operate in line with UAE and Dubai security regulations.
